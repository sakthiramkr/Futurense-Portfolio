YouTube Series · Content Strategy · Search-First

TrainerCentral Ignite — Scripting a YouTube Series for Indian Creators

YouTube is how hundreds of millions of Indians learn new tools. The brief for this series wasn't "make tutorial videos" — it was to build a content series structured around how Indian educators, trainers, and creators actually search on the platform.

Every episode was scripted around a specific search intent: not what TrainerCentral wanted to explain, but what a trainer in India was trying to figure out. The difference is significant — it changes the episode title, the opening hook, the pacing, and what gets left out.

I wrote the scripts, structured the content hierarchy, and developed the episode briefs from search research. The series was designed for discoverability first, product education second — because an unseen tutorial helps nobody.

India Campaign · OOH + Digital · Local Cultural Register

The Vadivelu × Chai Kings Campaign — Writing for Chennai, Not for India

Most "India-localised" content is global copy with the INR symbol swapped in. This campaign was built the other way: started with the city, the cultural reference, and the platform where the audience actually lives, then worked backwards to the message.

The hook was Vadivelu — Tamil cinema's most recognisable comedian — in the context of Chai Kings, a Chennai tea shop chain that had become a genuine cultural touchstone. The copy didn't need to explain the reference. It just needed to earn it.

I wrote all campaign copy: OOH taglines, digital ad scripts, social captions, and the follow-up email sequences. I also led creative direction for the visual assets — briefing and working with in-house designers and video editors to ensure the visuals matched the cultural register of the copy. The campaign only works if both halves speak the same language. The brief was to drive trial signups from solo trainers and independent creators in Tamil Nadu. The result was a 40% lift in direct traffic — the clearest possible signal that the audience recognised the campaign as one of their own.

Campaign Creative — Vadivelu × Chai Kings

The insight

Vadivelu and Chai Kings aren't India references. They're Chennai references. That specificity is why it landed — the audience felt seen, not targeted.

The copy principle

The best local content assumes the audience already belongs to the culture. It doesn't introduce it — it earns it.

The result

40% direct traffic lift. Direct traffic is the clearest brand signal — people typed the URL because they remembered the campaign.

Market Localisation · Singapore · TAE/WSQ

Singapore TAE/WSQ — Writing for a Regulated Training Market You Don't Live In

The brief was to launch TrainerCentral in Singapore's professional training market — a segment dominated by government-recognised certifications, regulated frameworks (TAE, WSQ), and buyers who speak a very specific industry language.

The existing TrainerCentral content was written for independent creators in India. I couldn't just translate it. I had to learn the Singapore training market's vocabulary from scratch — how their practitioners describe their work, what credentials they care about, which certifications they reference in conversation — and rewrite every surface from that starting point.

Landing pages, ad creative, Google Ads RSAs, social content: all rewritten with zero global-defaults. The result was meaningful traffic and signup growth from a standing start — validation that local vocabulary, not translated global copy, was the lever.

Led localisation strategy for India across Tamil and Hindi — adapting product messaging, campaign creative, and UX copy for regional audiences rather than running global assets through a translator. The difference shows immediately: translated copy reads like translated copy.

Hindi

Content Engine · SEO · Community · 4 Years

Building a Content Engine That Drives 43% of Inbound — Solo

No content team. No agency. No budget for a writer. Every article, comparison page, Reddit post, case study, and community thread written by one PMM over four years — and it became the primary acquisition channel for TrainerCentral globally.

The engine runs across four content types: SEO comparison articles (e.g. "TrainerCentral vs Kajabi") that intercept mid-funnel buyers already evaluating; Reddit community threads in creator and L&D subreddits that drive 12% conversion; case studies written around vertical-specific outcomes; and landing pages for every audience segment, market, and use case.

The discipline here isn't writing volume. It's knowing which surface serves which stage of the buyer's journey, and writing content that answers the right question at the right moment — not content that talks about the product.
